# 🎮 Legend Card - User Guide

## What is the Legend Card?

The **Legend Card** is the final screen of the onboarding flow where your player profile transforms into a premium, shareable card with 3D effects and dynamic stats visualization.

---

## 🎯 How to Use

### **Step 1: View Your Card**
After completing position and role selection, you'll see your personalized Legend Card featuring:
- Your nickname in bold uppercase
- Your selected avatar
- Your position and specific role
- Your overall rating (calculated from 6 stats)
- Individual stat bars with rarity-based colors

### **Step 2: Interact with the Card**
- **Drag/Pan**: Touch and drag anywhere on the card to tilt it in 3D
- **Watch Animations**: 
  - Glossy shine sweeps across the card
  - Overall rating pulsates with a glow effect
  - Stat bars animate on load

### **Step 3: Adjust Your Stats** ⚙️
1. Tap the **"AJUSTAR ATRIBUTOS"** button below the card
2. A bottom sheet slides up with 6 stat sliders:
   - **RIT** (Ritmo/Pace)
   - **TIR** (Tiro/Shooting)
   - **PAS** (Pase/Passing)
   - **DEF** (Defensa/Defense)
   - **FIS** (Físico/Physical)
   - **AGU** (Aguante/Stamina)
3. Slide each stat from 0-10
4. Watch the card update in real-time with new colors
5. Tap the **checkmark icon** or tap outside to close

### **Step 4: Enter Photo Mode** 📸
1. Tap the **camera icon** in the top-right corner
2. The screen cleans up for a perfect screenshot:
   - All buttons disappear
   - Background blurs to stadium image
   - Only the card and minimal branding remain
3. Take a screenshot using your device's native method:
   - **iOS**: Volume Up + Power Button
   - **Android**: Volume Down + Power Button
4. Tap the **X button** to exit Photo Mode

### **Step 5: Confirm Your Profile** ✅
1. When satisfied with your stats, tap **"¡CONFIRMAR FICHAJE!"**
2. Your profile is saved and you're taken to the main app

## 🎨 Understanding Rarity Colors

Your stats are color-coded based on their value:

| Value | Rarity | Color | Label |
|-------|--------|-------|-------|
| 9-10 | **ELITE** | 🟡 Gold (`#FFD700`) | NIVEL SELECCIÓN |
| 7-8 | **PRO** | 🟣 Purple (`#A855F7`) | CRACK DE BARRIO |
| 5-6 | **AMATEUR** | 🔵 Cyan (`#00EAFF`) | PROMESA LOCAL |
| 0-4 | **BASE** | ⚪ Grey (`#E5E4E2`) | ASPIRANTE |

**Your overall rating** (top-left number) also changes color based on the average of all 6 stats.

## 🏆 Card Elements Explained

### **Overall Rating (Top-Left)**
- Large number showing your average stat value
- Pulsates with a glow effect
- Color matches your rarity tier

### **Position Badge (Top-Right)**
- Shows your main position (DEL, MED, DEF, ARQ)
- White text on semi-transparent background

### **Avatar (Center)**
- Your selected player avatar
- Scaled up for visual impact
- Falls back to icon if none selected

### **Player Info**
- **Nickname**: Your chosen name in bold
- **Role**: Your specific position (e.g., "DELANTERO CENTRO")

### **Stat Bars**
- 6 horizontal bars showing each attribute
- Animated fill from 0% to current value
- Color-coded by individual stat value
- Number displayed on the right

### **Metallic Seal (Bottom)**
- Silver gradient badge
- Shows your rarity tier label
- Seal icon for authenticity

### **Glossy Effect**
- Diagonal light ray animation
- Continuously sweeps across the card
- Adds premium feel

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### **Card doesn't tilt when I drag**
- Make sure you're dragging directly on the card area
- Try a slower, more deliberate pan gesture

### **Stats don't update when I move sliders**
- Close and reopen the bottom sheet
- Make sure you're moving the slider thumb, not tapping

### **Photo Mode doesn't hide all UI**
- Wait for the fade animation to complete (~300ms)
- Try tapping the camera icon again

### **Avatar doesn't show**
- Go back and reselect your avatar in the previous screen
- Make sure you tapped "Continuar" after selecting

### **Colors look wrong**
- Check your stat values - colors are based on the number
- Restart the app if colors seem stuck
